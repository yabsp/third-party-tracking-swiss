\section{Introduction}
Third-party tracking has been studied extensively~\cite{englehardtOnlineTracking1millionsite2016,mayerThirdPartyWebTracking2012}.
This paper addresses the following research questions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{RQ1:} Do Swiss websites track visitors before consent more often than comparable German websites?
  \item \textbf{RQ2:} How does tracking differ across Swiss sectors?
  \item \textbf{RQ3:} Which companies receive the data?
\end{itemize}
